---
title: "Test your ads with Meta"
description: "Let Meta compare the images and videos in an ad set and tell you which works best"
---

When an ad set has two to five images or videos, Aventor can ask Meta to run its own creative test on them. Meta shows each one evenly, measures which performs best, and Aventor shows you the result. The test is optional and is off unless you turn it on.

## Turn on a test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the media step">
    Each Meta ad set group shows its images and videos under **Creative media**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Test these with Meta">
    The switch appears on Meta groups with two to five images or videos. You'll see **Meta creative test on · starts when your campaign starts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch and start your campaign">
    Aventor saves your choice at launch. The test begins when your campaign starts running, so it never runs while the campaign is paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't change the switch after the campaign is launched.

## What the test costs

The test runs for **7 days** and uses part of the ad set's existing budget. It never adds spend. While it runs, Meta splits that budget evenly between your ads instead of favouring the one it picked early.

## Read the result

On your campaign's analytics page, the **Ad sets and ads** card shows the test:

- **Creative test running · ends** with the end date while it runs.
- **Top performer** with Meta's confidence on the winning ad once Meta has a result.
- **Meta found no clear winner** when no ad did clearly better. This is common for small budgets, because Meta needs enough results to be sure.

## Nothing changes on its own

Aventor never pauses, edits or deletes an ad because of a test. You decide what to do next, for example pausing the weaker ads or making more ads like the winner.

<Note>
  Creative tests need a Meta ad account that belongs to a Meta Business. If yours doesn't, the card tells you the test isn't available for that ad account, and your campaign runs as usual.
</Note>
