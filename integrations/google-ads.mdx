Google Ads campaign launch is rolling out through Aventor's launch workflow. Before campaign parameters are prepared for review, Aventor checks your connected Google Ads account data first and asks only for the setup decisions it cannot verify.

## Account connection and customer selection

Connect Google Ads from the Aventor launch flow, then select the customer account Aventor should use for campaign preparation and launch.

What Aventor checks:

- A Google Ads connection exists for your Aventor account.
- A customer account is selected.
- The selected customer can be read by Aventor with the granted OAuth permissions.
- Basic account readiness, such as account status and billing visibility, can be verified when Google Ads exposes it.

If the connection or customer selection is missing, Aventor pauses launch preparation and sends you back to the existing connect/select account flow. Campaign parameter drafts are not generated until the required Google Ads and Meta account setup is complete.

## Conversion tracking readiness

For conversion-focused goals, Aventor checks Google Ads conversion readiness before generating launch-ready parameters. This includes enabled primary conversion actions and biddable customer conversion goals when the Google Ads API can read them.

If conversion tracking cannot be verified, Aventor treats it as a warning, not an account-setup blocker. You can:

- switch to a traffic-focused goal;
- continue with the warning if you are comfortable reviewing the campaign anyway; or
- pause and set up conversion tracking in Google Ads first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open conversion goals">
    In Google Ads, open Goals or Conversions from your selected customer account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or verify a conversion action">
    Confirm that the website, lead, purchase, or other relevant action is enabled and marked primary when it should drive bidding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify biddable goals">
    Confirm the customer conversion goal for the action category is biddable if you want Google to optimize for conversions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry Aventor launch readiness">
    Return to Aventor and continue launch preparation after setup is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
If Google Ads permissions or provider availability prevent Aventor from reading conversion data, Aventor records the status as unknown or unavailable instead of claiming tracking is missing.
</Info>

## Google Business Profile readiness

Google Business Profile is optional enrichment for local businesses. When your campaign appears local or a Business Profile selection already exists, Aventor checks for a saved Google Business Profile location.

- A selected Business Profile is a positive readiness fact and can help local campaign context.
- Missing Business Profile access, missing scope, or no selected location does not block campaign parameter generation.
- For clearly non-local campaigns with no Business Profile signal, Aventor skips this guidance.

If you want to use Business Profile context, connect Google with Business Profile access and select the relevant location in Aventor when prompted.

## Meta Pixel link from launch warnings

Meta Pixel readiness is documented separately. When a Meta warning appears, use [Meta Pixel setup](/integrations/pixel-setup) for setup guidance.

## What you'll need

- Google Ads account access.
- A selected Google Ads customer account.
- Billing configured in Google Ads when you intend to launch.
- Conversion actions/goals for conversion-focused campaigns.
- Optional: Google Business Profile access and a selected location for local-business enrichment.
