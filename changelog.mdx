<Update label="October 8, 2026" description="v1.0.2" tags={["Release"]}>
  ## Version 1.0.2

  - **Book a demo.** Schedule a demo with the Aventor team right from the website.
  - **Redesigned landing page**, and the headline types itself in again.
  - **Platform previews show your business.** Ad previews use your own name, details and website logo instead of
    placeholders, including after you reload.
  - **Ad images in Meta analytics.** Each ad row on the Meta analytics page now shows the ad's image, and the page
    reads more clearly.
  - **Meta budget-raise recommendations** now show how much extra you'd spend each day.
  - **Easier account setup.** The Connect Facebook and Connect Google Ads buttons are easier to spot.
  - **Simpler Meta website conversion setup.** Aventor no longer asks for 7 days of Pixel activity, and the final
    review no longer says your Pixel is missing when it's set up.
  - **Image versions stick.** The version you pick stays selected in the Design panel and the preview, and a failed
    image edit no longer shows "generating" forever.
  - **Add media after "No media for now".** Finalizing a plan with no media no longer leaves Submit failing, and you
    can add media again from the review.
  - **Billing** shows the right price for accounts that use an access code.
  - **More reliable Google campaigns.** The dashboard no longer offers Start after Google denies access, uploaded
    images attach to Performance Max ad sets, and schedule problems on the Google review are explained so you can
    fix them.
  - **More reliable Meta campaigns.** Saved audiences that have expired are no longer offered, browsing audiences for
    retargeting works on Meta + Google sessions, the Business Name field keeps your spaces, and a website link you
    edit on the Review panel is kept after the budget is regenerated.
  - Error messages now appear in the chat, reloading at the media step no longer spins, readiness errors are clearer,
    the AVENTOR logo takes you to your dashboard when you're signed in, and creating an account shows its progress.
</Update>

<Update label="October 6, 2026" description="v1.0.1" tags={["Release"]}>
  ## Version 1.0.1

  - **Daily automatic optimization.** Every morning Aventor applies the Meta and Google recommendations that don't
    raise your spend and moves budget between your campaigns, keeping your total the same. Turn it on or off for
    each launch from your dashboard.
  - **Redesigned analytics pages.** Daily charts for 7, 30 or 90 days, a timeline of changes, your Meta and Google
    scores, and a "What changed this week" summary that shows every budget move and why.
  - **Daily summary email** of the changes Aventor made. Turn it on or off under **Settings → Notifications**.
  - **Review your campaign setup before ads are built.** The plan opens in the right panel with a tab for each
    platform. Rename ad sets, adjust Meta age ranges and add or remove Search keywords, then approve it or ask for
    changes.
  - **Choose how your campaign starts.** When you submit for review, pick whether it starts automatically once
    approved or you start it yourself. The submit screen shows your daily budget for each platform and the total.
  - **Access codes.** Have an access code? Enter it in the billing step to use Aventor free, with no card needed.
  - **Refreshed dashboard** with clearer status labels and campaign rows that show each campaign's daily budget.
  - **Replace a blocked image.** If an image can't be generated, upload your own in its place and keep going.
  - **More reliable launches.** Fixes for Meta campaigns that target a city or a whole state, narrow age ranges and
    saved-audience exclusions, for Google campaigns whose connection had expired, for starting again after a pause,
    and for uploaded customer lists that never finished syncing.
  - **Better Google Search keyword ideas** for businesses that used to get none.
  - Your Review panel edits are kept when you submit, a Google link that can't be used is flagged instead of
    dropped, and reloading while your plan is being drafted keeps the progress screen.
</Update>
