New releases are listed here, newest first. Each production release gets a version number.

<Update label="October 2, 2026" description="v1.0.0" tags={["Release"]}>
  ## Version 1.0

  Aventor now ships in numbered releases, and this page lists what each one changes. Version 1.0 is the starting
  point and includes everything below.

  - **Release notes in the app.** Open your profile menu and choose **Release notes** to come here.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026">
  - **One inbox for all your leads.** Meta and Google leads in one place, with filters, a viewed status and CSV export.
  - **Conversion tracking setup.** Aventor checks your Meta and Google conversion tracking before launch and can set
    up what's missing.
  - **Clearer billing.** See your payment status, with help when a payment fails.
  - **Reference images.** Pick images from your media and say whether each one must appear in the ad or is only
    inspiration.
  - **Easier review.** The review page is grouped by step, with a **Change** button for each platform, and shows your
    campaign type and conversions.
  - **New pricing page** and a refreshed website.
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026">
  - **Lead forms.** Build a Meta Instant Form or a Google lead form in Aventor, add your own questions, and attach it
    to a campaign.
  - **Leads tab** on your dashboard and on each campaign.
  - **Subscription plans** with a free trial, paid through Stripe.
  - **Quick summary of your brief** that you can approve or correct before the campaign is built.
  - **Location targeting** by radius, postal code or region.
  - **Choose the image to edit from** when you ask for changes.
</Update>

<Update label="July 2026">
  - **Guided tour** the first time you open Aventor.
  - **Rebuilt campaign planning and media steps.**
  - **More accurate campaign analytics**, with clear currency and empty states.
  - Unsaved uploads can no longer be picked for launch, and Meta Pixel problems are explained more clearly.
</Update>

<Update label="June 2026">
  - **Pre-launch check** of your ad accounts, Meta Pixel, Google conversions and (optionally) your Google Business
    Profile.
  - **Campaign duration and scheduling** controls.
  - **Help for Google users without an Ads account**, and a smoother Google connection.
  - Uploaded images stay editable, and Meta Pixel problems at launch can be fixed and retried.
</Update>

<Update label="May 2026">
  - **Edit how your budget is split** before launch; your changes survive regeneration.
  - **Google Business Profile** context and a map preview of your Google locations.
  - **Better Google Search keywords.**
  - **Sharper image editing**, with queued edit versions you can follow.
  - Expired Google connections are shown clearly so you can reconnect.
</Update>

<Update label="April 2026">
  - **Waitlist** for new users.
  - **Pick up where you left off**: your campaign progress is restored when you come back.
</Update>

<Update label="March 2026">
  - **Build campaigns by chatting.** A new AI assistant plans your campaign, connects your ad accounts and launches,
    all inside the conversation.
  - **Website scan** pre-fills your business details.
  - **Ongoing campaigns** with a monthly budget.
  - **Email notifications** about your campaigns.
</Update>

<Update label="February 2026">
  - **Recommendations explained**: the AI tells you why it suggests each setting.
  - **Follow-up questions** before your campaign settings are drafted.
  - **Budget optimization** across your Meta and Google campaigns.
</Update>

<Update label="January 2026">
  - **Total campaign budget.**
  - **File attachments.**
  - Meta Pixel fix and better handling when ad platforms are busy.
</Update>

<Update label="December 2025">
  - **Google Ads launch**: Search and Performance Max campaigns.
  - **Campaign analytics** on your dashboard.
  - Reworked ad creation.
</Update>

<Update label="November 2025">
  - **The new Aventor web app**, with privacy and terms pages.
</Update>

## Earlier

### January 2025

#### Meta Ads Integration

- **Launch campaigns directly** to Facebook and Instagram
- OAuth-based connection to Meta Business accounts
- Select ad account and page for each campaign
- Automatic placement optimization

#### AI Image Editing

- **Natural language editing** - describe changes and AI applies them
- Create variations from existing images
- Iterative refinement with edit history

#### Multi-Provider Generation

- Generate images from multiple AI providers simultaneously
- Automatic selection of best results
- Support for DALL-E 3, Imagen 3, and Gemini
